Independent project · 2026

Relay

I built Relay independently as a voice AI receptionist and unified inbox for small businesses. It answers calls, books appointments, and connects callers with staff when needed.

  • OpenAI Realtime API
  • Telnyx
  • AgentOS
  • pgvector
  • Stripe

View the demo →

  • About 300 ms voice response latency, with live Google Calendar booking and warm transfers that brief staff before connecting callers.
  • A knowledge base combines vector and keyword search across websites, files, Google Drive and APIs. Publishing controls and factual guardrails govern what the agent can say, while rules handle call routing and business hours.
  • One inbox for six channels: calls, web chat, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp and SMS, with transcripts and summaries.
  • Stripe usage-based credit billing and self-serve onboarding to a first test call in under 10 minutes.
